  • Pre-measured Njavara Raw Grains: Red medicinal rice for cellular tissue repair.
  • Natural Herbal Spice Kizhi: Dashapushpam, Trikatu, fenugreek & garden cress seeds.

The 4-Step Home Brewing Ritual

7 Days of Gut Reset
Step 1: Rinse 1 pouch of pure unpolished Njavara rice in clean water.
Step 2: Add 8 cups of water and submerge the pre-tied Herbal Spice Kizhi.
Step 3: Slow-simmer for 35 minutes until the medicinal starch tenderizes.
Step 4: Temper with fresh coconut milk and ghee; consume warm for dinner.
